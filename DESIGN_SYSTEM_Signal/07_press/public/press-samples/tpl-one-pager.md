## Key Architecture Controls

1. **Move-Then-Confirm Publish:** Staging nodes move into target market containers before status flips to Published.
2. **Agent Lockdown:** Frontline seats use structured bot commands rather than direct register cell edits.
3. **T+24h Verification Re-Scan:** Corrections remain provisional until verified clean on the next nightly pass.

> **KEY GOVERNANCE INVARIANT:** Leaving unverified policy guidance in active circulation past Day 30 is treated as a higher compliance risk than an archived slot.
