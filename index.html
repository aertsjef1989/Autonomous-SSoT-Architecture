Pillar I: SSoT Highlights ([Status: Designed + Prototype])

The Constraint

Designed under strict enterprise compliance blockers that prohibited standard off-the-shelf Knowledge Management Systems (such as Confluence, Zendesk KMS, or Salesforce Knowledge).

The Architecture

Specified as a zero-license-cost knowledge operations system ([Status: Designed], interactive flows [Status: Prototype]) built on standard enterprise workspace primitives (Enterprise Wiki, Relational Governance Base, and ChatOps Bot). The reference model spans 40 markets and 3,055 public reference nodes (3,000+ nodes, Chapter 2: System Architecture) using a normalized 11-table schema (Chapter 4: Relational Database Schema) and 14 conversational bot commands (Chapter 6: Bot Command Reference).

Verification & Business Case (Standardized Lean Reference Model)

Modeled using Lean Six Sigma DMADV methodology on a standardized 100-seat enterprise reference scenario, yielding a modeled gross recoverable value range of EUR 198,000 to EUR 297,000 / year (40% to 60% friction recovery; EUR 188,100 to EUR 287,100 / year net after illustrative EUR 9,900 / year maintenance; Chapter 1). Governance specifies 10 automated controls (5 scheduled scanners, 5 event detectors) plus an external dead-man heartbeat (HB-001, Chapter 5), 5 Internal Container Access Tiers (I-0 to I-4) across 6 Operating Roles (Chapter 7), and a 98.2% (~98%) reduction in metered platform automation runs.

Pillar II: Balaerion Highlights ([Status: Prototype + Designed])

The Constraint

Teams adopting free-tier or multi-vendor LLM endpoints routinely expose unmasked client identifiers across non-EU providers, hardcode rotating model IDs, or allow generative prose to invent company registration facts.

The Architecture

Architected as a dual-mode serverless AI gateway (Mode A 12-provider standalone workbench + Mode B ProspectHub 7-state GTM workspace; Balaerion I) routing 23 AI personas (7 Mode A utilities + 16 Mode B specialists; Balaerion IV) across strict lane: eu and lane: general boundaries (Balaerion III). ProspectHub enriches accounts via a Hybrid 8-Collector Card-Free OSINT Layer (3 zero-key built-in collectors for DNS/SPF/DMARC, HTML CMS/Pixel/CMP head scan, and FR SIREN Open API + 5 optional free-tier collectors; Balaerion V) and compiles grounded briefs directly into Signal Studio (16:9) and Signal Press (A4) (Balaerion VI).

Verification & Safeguards

Pre-dispatch regex and lane guards block VAT, IBAN, email, and eu -> general cross-lane leaks (HTTP 403) before any socket opens; throttled eu endpoints fail closed to deterministic Replay rather than crossing lanes; and public calibration fixtures use synthetic Belgian KBO 0123.456.789 (which intentionally fails mod-97 check 49 != 89 so it can never match a real company). Explore the recorded sandbox in the Balaerion Interactive Demo.

Pillar III: Syndicate Highlights ([Status: Prototype (Simulation) + Designed])

The Constraint

Group sports prediction pools and recreational tipgames suffer from two structural defects: intuition-driven picks ignore expected-goals (xG) variance, low-score draw clustering, and bookmaker overround skew, while shared spreadsheets silently dilute existing participants whenever members enter or exit mid-season.

The Architecture

Designed as a strictly non-financial statistical simulation and unit-accounting system (Syndicate I): a Dixon-Coles (1997) correlated Poisson scoreline engine paired with Shin (1993) insider overround de-vigging (Syndicate II), a quorum-gated selection card state machine with a hard T - 15m pre-kickoff lock (Syndicate III), a fixed-scale NUMERIC(18,6) unit-share Net Asset Value (NAV) ledger denominated in non-monetary virtual units (VU) (Syndicate IV), and proper scoring calibration (Brier, Log-Loss, CLV) plus 1,000-path Monte Carlo Quarter-Kelly drawdown stress testing (Syndicate V).

Verification & Safeguards

Enforces inter-matchday dealing cutoffs (blocking stale-NAV entry while selection cards are in play), ROUND_HALF_EVEN rounding with residual sweep, UNIQUE (idempotency_key) settlement protection, and PostgreSQL REVOKE UPDATE, DELETE triggers. Exercise the 36-club synthetic league table, Dixon-Coles + Shin lab, 1,000-run Monte Carlo simulator, and VU ledger in the Syndicate Simulation Demo.
