Gambling & Real Money Games

PERISHABLE CLAIM STALENESS
This document contains regulatory thresholds that are overdue for verification. Proceed with caution.
1. General Prohibition
The promotion of unlicensed gambling, real money games, and simulated casino games is strictly prohibited in the DE market.
2. Licensed Operators
Entities holding a valid license from the Gemeinsame Glücksspielbehörde der Länder (GGL) may advertise, subject to strict targeting restrictions (18+ only, no targeting of vulnerable groups).
3. Pre-Approval SLA
All gambling creatives require manual compliance review. Pre-approval takes 15 to 20 working days. Campaigns must not be scheduled to launch before written approval is attached to the ticket.
